Safety Center

Everyday habits that protect you

Good habits stop most scams before they get anywhere near your money.

🔐 OTP, PIN, and passwords are yours alone

No bank, government office, or company will ever call or message you asking for your OTP, ATM PIN, or password. Ever. Not even "to verify your identity."

⏱ Slow down when there's urgency

"Act within 24 hours or your account is blocked" is a manufactured deadline. Genuine issues give you real time to verify calmly.

🔗 Don't click, navigate directly

Instead of tapping a link in a message, open your bank's official app or type the website address yourself.

🎁 If you didn't enter, you can't win

Prizes, lucky draws, and lotteries you never entered are not real — regardless of how official the message looks.

💸 Refunds never need your PIN

Receiving money — a refund, cashback, or payment — never requires you to enter your UPI PIN. PINs are only for sending money.

🤐 Be wary of "don't tell anyone"

If a caller or message asks you to keep something secret from your bank or family, that's a manipulation tactic — not a legitimate request.

📞 Verify helplines independently

Look up customer-care numbers from the company's official app or website — not from a message, ad, or search result.

👴 Look out for elderly family members

Digital-arrest and fake-police scams disproportionately target older adults. Talk through common scam patterns with your family.
