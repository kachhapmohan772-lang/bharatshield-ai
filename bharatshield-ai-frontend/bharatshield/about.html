Why BharatShield AI exists

The problem

Every day, ordinary Indian users receive SMS, WhatsApp messages, and emails designed to look official — fake KYC alerts, prize notifications, courier fees, refund claims. Many people can't easily tell whether a message is genuine or fraudulent, and the decision often has to be made in seconds, under pressure.

Our approach

BharatShield AI doesn't just say "this is a scam." It follows one principle throughout:

RISK → WHY → EVIDENCE → ACTION

Every result shows the risk level, the specific signals that led to it, and clear next steps — so you understand the reasoning, not just a verdict.

What this prototype is — and isn't

This is a rule-based prototype built for a digital-safety competition. It analyzes text patterns known to be associated with common Indian scam formats. It is not a certified fraud-detection system, and it does not claim perfect accuracy. A clean result never guarantees safety, and a flagged result is never proof of fraud — it is a prompt to verify.

What's next

The architecture is built so a real backend, a trained AI/NLP model, Hindi and regional-language support, and a browser extension can be added later without redesigning the product from scratch.
