Privacy & Disclaimer

How your data is handled

What we process

Messages, links, and screenshot text you submit are analyzed entirely in your browser using local pattern-matching logic. In this prototype, nothing is uploaded to a server.

What we store

Your most recent result is kept temporarily in your browser's session storage so it can be shown on the Result page. It is automatically cleared when you close the tab. We never store OTPs, PINs, passwords, or other credentials — even if you accidentally paste them.

What we don't do

  • We don't send your message or screenshot content to any external server in this prototype.
  • We don't visit, open, or execute any link you submit.
  • We don't track you across other websites.

Disclaimer

BharatShield provides an AI-assisted risk assessment and is not a definitive determination of fraud. It is a rule-based prototype built for a digital-safety competition, and it can occasionally miss a real scam (false negative) or flag legitimate content (false positive). Always verify important information — anything involving money, OTPs, or personal documents — through official, independently-sourced channels.

If you need help

If you've already shared sensitive details or lost money, contact your bank's official helpline immediately, and report the incident to India's National Cyber Crime Helpline at 1930 or via cybercrime.gov.in.
